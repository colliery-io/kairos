# Strategy Level Overview

## The Board

This is where strategic execution lives. One board, owned by the strategy team (leadership). The board contains **Strategies** — the concrete, coordinated bets the organization is making right now.

The **Company Vision** informs this board but doesn't live on it. This is the "why the company exists" — the boundary that all strategies are evaluated against. Not a ticket, not a column. The board is a living work area — everything on it should be actively flowing.

Columns: **Draft → Review → Active → Monitoring → Completed**. Strategies flow left-to-right. Every strategy is a hypothesis: "we're doing X because we believe Y will happen." Monitoring is the post-execution phase where you track whether Y actually occurred — validating or invalidating the hypothesis before closing it out.

## Ceremonies

### Strategy Review — Monthly (or Quarterly)

**Who leads:** Strategy team lead
**Who participates:** Strategy team, initiative coordinators (as needed)

This is a single meeting that covers both reviewing what's in flight and pulling new work in. Walk the board right-to-left.

**Actions that come out:**

- Monitoring strategies evaluated — hypothesis validated (move to Completed) or invalidated (retire with learnings captured)
- Active strategies assessed — pivot, continue, or move to Monitoring
- Draft/Review items evaluated — promote, send back, or kill
- New strategies pulled into Active when initiative capacity exists (JIT replenishment)
- Updated risk levels on strategies where conditions have changed
- Stale drafts archived (if sitting untouched > 1 quarter, question whether it belongs)

### Strategy–Initiative Sync — Monthly

**Who leads:** Strategy team lead
**Who participates:** Initiative coordinators

Coordinators report on how their initiatives are tracking against active strategies. This is a lightweight checkpoint, not a deep dive.

**Actions that come out:**

- Strategy-to-initiative alignment confirmed or corrected
- Misaligned or orphaned initiatives flagged for re-scoping
- New initiative proposals surfaced for upcoming strategy capacity
- Blockers escalated that require strategic-level decisions
